Help Center

Common questions and troubleshooting.

How does it read my screen
EndXL reads your screen by capturing end to end encrypted screenshots sent to an A.I. using your provided API Key

How can I fix repeated incorrect answers
If you recieve an incorrect answer more than 3 times in a row, hold CTRL + SHFT + R for 3 seconds and then release. If this doesn't work create a support ticket

How does the A.I. recieve the screenshots?
Your screenshots are sent to the A.I. through your custom API Key which is attached to your Google Account. We never see any of your personal information

Is my data safe?
Yes. All personal information is encrypted before being sent to the A.I. ensuring anything on the webpage is kept safe and secure

Do I need to make an account
No accounts are needed to use EndXL to its maximum capacity. EndXL will remain free for all users forever
